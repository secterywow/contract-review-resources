# Contract Review Resources

A simple collection of resources for reading, reviewing, and comparing contracts in plain language.

## Free AI Contract Review Tool

[SpotClause Free AI Contract Review Tool](https://spotclause.app/free-ai-contract-review-tool) helps summarize contracts, identify key clauses, and explain important terms in plain language.

It can help with:

- key clauses
- cancellation terms
- renewal terms
- payment terms
- obligations
- deadlines
- contract summaries

SpotClause is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice.

## Contract Clause Library

[Contract Clause Library](https://spotclause.app/contract-clauses)

Plain-language explanations of common contract clauses, including cancellation clauses, renewal clauses, payment terms, termination clauses, notice periods, confidentiality, liability, and more.

## Contract Comparison

[Compare Contracts](https://spotclause.app/compare-contracts)

Compare two contract versions and see added, removed, changed, and unchanged wording.
